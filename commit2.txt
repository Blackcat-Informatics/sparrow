Stop listing an artifact as held once expiry has released its bytes (#323)

Review finding. Expiry retires an artifact's chunks at its deadline and keeps
the entry a day longer. The payload index looked only at the evicted flags, so
for that day a lookup listed an artifact whose bytes were already released.

Change. One predicate, manifest_retire::releases_bytes (evicted, or any chunk
retired), decides the index key in both the batch owner and the migration page.
The age-out test now expires the artifact at its deadline, before the extra day,
and checks the lookup is empty and the payload-index counter equals its recount.
payload_index::digest now goes through import::valid_payload_id instead of a
second validator, and PayloadLookup is must_use. The module doc and the
architecture and interfaces text now say the index is stricter than the
per-source counters, whose completeness looks only at the evicted flags and has
the same day-long gap; they are not changed here.
