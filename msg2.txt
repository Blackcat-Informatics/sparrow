Check each record's bytes against its link digest on decode (#327)

Cause: review found the claim that extract's bytes are checked against an
archived digest was false. The PurRDF reader stores a blob under its
declared digest without hashing the decompressed bytes, and nothing compared
them with the `payloadBlake3` link, so altered blob bytes in an internally
consistent container would have extracted.

Change: the decoder now hashes each record's bytes with digest_string and
refuses the segment, naming the file, when it differs from the link digest;
extract and verify both fail on it. Section 9 of docs/archive-format.md now
says what the reader checks (per-record digest, contiguous records, total
equal to byteLength, no whole-artifact BLAKE3 comparison), and the usage
paragraph says a damaged segment anywhere blocks extraction. New test builds
a consistent segment with PurRDF's public writer, honest and forged.

Not verified: the `payloadSha256` link is still not compared; the stored
format is unchanged.
