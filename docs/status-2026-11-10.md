## 8. Razor and mandate (recorded)

- Sparrow is a low-assurance, high-throughput, searchable, centralized log store. It optimizes only for speed and storage efficiency.
- Razor: long-term storage, privacy, encryption, validation or resilience goes to Katamari at a high, justified bar; everything else goes to Sparrow. Nothing irreplaceable may have its only copy in Sparrow.
- Radical improvement: Sparrow beats scattered log files that may be altered, reaped, accidentally deleted or pruned. Being in one place, searchable, compact and fast is already the win.
- Consume watches: sparrow-ship reads originals and copies them; soul originals remain in place. The katamari journal lines are log data and stay in Sparrow, per the coordinator.
